# Rail Shebok — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 15 October 2026

## 1. What Rail Shebok does

Rail Shebok is a helper application for booking Bangladesh Railway e-tickets. The
app renders the official e-ticketing service of Bangladesh Railway inside the app
so you can book your own tickets faster. Rail Shebok is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or sponsored by Bangladesh Railway.

## 2. Data we collect and how it is used

| Data | Where it lives | How it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Railway account mobile number & session token | On **this device only** (app storage) | To keep you logged in to the official railway service |
| Passenger name & phone number (as required by the railway booking form) | Sent **only to the official railway service** during booking | To complete your booking |
| SMS OTP codes | Read **locally** via the Android notification-listener, held in memory only | Only to auto-fill the railway's verification box; never stored, never sent anywhere else |
| Device / advertising identifiers (AAID) | Processed by the advertising SDK (only when ads are enabled) | To serve and measure advertisements |
| Anonymous technical logs (network errors, timing) | On this device only | To diagnose booking failures |

## 3. What we do NOT do

- We do **not** operate any account system of our own.
- We do **not** upload, sell, or share your personal data with anyone. The only
  recipient of your booking data is the official Bangladesh Railway e-ticketing
  service, which is required to complete your booking.
- We do **not** store your password. Your password is typed by you into the
  official railway service's own login page.

## 4. Third-party services

- **Bangladesh Railway e-ticketing service** — the official ticketing service;
  its own privacy terms apply when you log in and book there.
- **Advertising SDK (AppLovin MAX)** — used only when the in-app advertising is
  enabled; the SDK may process your device's advertising identifier to serve
  ads. AppLovin's own privacy policy applies: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/

## 5. Permissions

- **Notifications / notification-listener access** — used only to detect the
  railway's OTP SMS for auto-fill. You may revoke this access at any time in
  Android Settings; the app then simply waits for you to type the code manually.

## 6. Children's privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13.
